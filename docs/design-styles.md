## Neoclassical

Orderly Greco-Roman revival: symmetry, stone, and a little gold.

Keywords: serif, columns, marble, gold accents, symmetry, muted color.

## Baroque

Heavy ornament and drama, meant to feel grand.

Keywords: flourishes, deep shadow, gold leaf, high contrast, rich pattern.

## Aurora

Soft moving color, like northern lights: glow, blur, and gentle waves.

Keywords: iridescent gradient, soft glow, translucent layers, organic waves.

## Ethereal

Light, airy, and faint, as if the piece is made of mist.

Keywords: pale color, gauzy overlay, low contrast, feathered shadow, open space.

## Filigree

Fine metal linework, like jewelry drawn into the layout.

Keywords: delicate lines, metal ink, curves, lace, monogram.

## Acanthus

Classical leaf ornament: vines, stone, and a formal natural flourish.

Keywords: leaf motifs, symmetrical vines, stone, green and gold.

## Anthropomorphic

Objects given a face, limbs, or a character.

Keywords: eyes, limbs on objects, rounded characters, friendly shapes.

## Pixel Art

Pictures built from visible pixels and a small set of colors.

Keywords: pixel grid, low resolution, limited palette, 8-bit or 16-bit.

## Conceptual Sketch

Looks like a working drawing: pencil, notes, and unfinished lines.

Keywords: pencil or ink, hatching, grey paper, annotations.

## Luxury Typography

The letters do the work. Spaced serifs, metal color, almost no other decoration.

Keywords: serif, wide tracking, gold or metal color, monochrome, custom letterforms.

## Japandi

Calm and nearly empty. Japanese restraint with Scandinavian softness.

Keywords: light wood, beige and grey, clean sans, few icons, natural material.

## Memphis

Loud 1980s geometry: clashing color, squiggles, and toy-like shapes.

Keywords: squiggles, primary colors, grids, block shapes.

## Bohemian

Layered, earthy, and collected, with pattern and handmade texture.

Keywords: rich texture, jewel tones, pattern, script, organic material.

## Shabby Chic

Faded vintage softness: worn white, pale florals, and delicate script.

Keywords: whitewash, faded floral, cursive, lace, pale pink and blue.

## Farmhouse / Cottagecore

Rural and slow: cloth, wood, flowers, and a handmade feel.

Keywords: floral, gingham, wood, worn finishes, serif or hand lettering.

## Victorian

Dense 19th-century ornament: dark color, gilt, and heavy floral pattern.

Keywords: ornate serif, damask, deep red and green, gilt frames.

## Art Deco

1920s geometry and glamour: sunbursts, gold, and sharp symmetry.

Keywords: gold, sunburst, symmetry, angles, jewel tones.

## Art Nouveau

Flowing plant lines, hand lettering, and stained-glass color.

Keywords: curves, florals, hand lettering, earthy color, stained glass.

## Mystical Western

Desert western signs mixed with moons, tarot marks, and leather.

Keywords: tarot marks, cactus, western serif, sun and moon, earth tones, leather.

## Kitsch

Ironic excess: too much pattern and shine, on purpose.

Keywords: clashing print, dated type, plastic shine, retro ads.

## Y2K

Late-1990s and early-2000s tech gloss: metal, pixels, and iridescent shine.

Keywords: chrome, pixel type, metal, iridescent gradient, matrix grid.

## Bauhaus

Useful geometry: primary colors, basic shapes, and a clear grid.

Keywords: grid, sans serif, red blue yellow, circle triangle square.

## Brutalism

Raw and blunt. Grey or black and white, hard edges, almost no polish.

Keywords: mono type, greyscale, harsh edges, solid blocks, plain buttons.

## Cybercore

Dense hacker and science-fiction styling: neon, glitch, and code.

Keywords: neon, glitch, code pattern, green on black, tech symbols.

## Synthwave

A 1980s neon future: grid horizon, VHS grain, purple and cyan.

Keywords: horizon grid, VHS, neon gradient, chrome type, purple and cyan.

## Vaporwave

A hazy 1980s and 1990s collage: statues, glitch, pastel pink and purple.

Keywords: pastel pink and purple, VHS glitch, classical busts, lo-fi texture.

## Pop Art

Comic and advertising energy: flat primaries, thick outlines, and dots.

Keywords: halftone dots, bold outline, primary color, speech bubbles.

## Bento Box

Content in tidy rounded compartments.

Keywords: rounded blocks, soft shadow, icon with a label, neutral color.

## Graffiti

Street paint: drips, concrete, and loose lettering.

Keywords: spray texture, drips, concrete, neon, freestyle type.

## Tenebrism

Almost all dark, with one strong light on the subject.

Keywords: black ground, spotlight, high contrast, rim light.

## Gothic

Medieval dark romance: pointed forms, blackletter, and deep color.

Keywords: blackletter, stained glass, stone, deep purple and black.

## Pointillism

The picture is made of many small dots of color.

Keywords: dot clusters, optical mix, soft painterly color, grain.

## Mixed Media

Several materials in one piece: photo, drawing, paper, and cutouts.

Keywords: cutouts, overlays, collage, paper texture.

## Steampunk

Victorian dress plus brass machines, gears, and steam.

Keywords: copper, cogs, leather, serif, smoke.

## Kawaii

Round, cute, and pastel, with simple faces and hand lettering.

Keywords: pastel, rounded icons, simple faces, blush, handwritten type.

## Coquette

Soft romantic styling: pink, ribbon, pearls, and small serifs.

Keywords: baby pink, pearls, ribbons, dainty serif, hearts.

## Surrealism

Dream logic: wrong scale, odd pairings, and scenes that cannot be real.

Keywords: odd pairings, melting or impossible forms, unexpected scale, empty dream space.

## Utilitarian

Useful first. A grid, plain type, muted color, and no ornament.

Keywords: grid, plain or mono type, muted tones, no decoration, direct labels.

## Mid-Century

1940s to 1960s modern: clean lines, warm retro color, and simple shapes.

Keywords: clean lines, retro color, boomerang shapes, sans serif.

## Scrapbook

A personal collage of paper, tape, photos, and handwriting.

Keywords: paper texture, tape, photo frames, hand lettering, stickers.

## Neo Frutiger Aero

Glossy early-web bubbles: aqua, soft transparency, and rounded icons.

Keywords: aqua, translucent bubbles, gloss, friendly icons, rounded interface.

## Dark Magic Academia

A dark school of symbols: candles, old books, black and gold, calligraphy.

Keywords: candles, old books, etchings, black and gold, serif script.

## Light Academia

Soft study and daylight: cream, books, linen, and quiet serifs.

Keywords: cream neutrals, serif, books, sunlight, linen.

## Wabi Sabi

Beauty in the worn and uneven. Earth color, rough texture, little ornament.

Keywords: earth tones, rough texture, natural light, organic form, quiet type.

## South West / Wild West

Desert and ranch: terracotta, denim, cactus, and a western serif.

Keywords: terracotta, cactus, denim, western serif, sun-baked ground.

## Nautical

Sea kit: navy and white, rope, stripes, and brass.

Keywords: navy and white, anchors, rope, stripes, brass, stencil or serif.

## Rebus

A picture stands in for a word or a sound.

Keywords: pictograms, visual puns, image mixed with type, clean layout.

## Glassmorphism

Frosted glass panels: blur, see-through color, and a soft shadow.

Keywords: frosted ground, blur, semi-transparent, soft shadow, light tint.

## Modular Typography

Letters treated as blocks on a grid, with even spacing.

Keywords: geometric letters, type grid, even spacing, built rhythm.

## Neo-Brutalism

Brutalism made usable: big type, a hard layout, bold color, and few parts.

Keywords: bold color, large type, stark layout, few components, deliberate asymmetry.
